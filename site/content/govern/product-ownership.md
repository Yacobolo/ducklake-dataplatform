# Product Ownership

Use this guide when a dataset is important enough to be shared as a product and supported over time.

## Inputs

- an owner team
- a domain
- a contract and support expectation
- an access request path

## Flow

1. assign an owner team and steward
2. document the intended audience and business purpose
3. define outputs and semantic entrypoints
4. publish the request path for access or support
5. review subscriptions, dependencies, versions, and scorecards over time

## What Good Looks Like

- consumers know who owns the product
- builders know what outputs are in contract
- operators know who to contact when freshness or runtime issues appear
